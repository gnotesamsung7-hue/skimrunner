How to Fly

Radio (Mode 2): left stick up/down = thrust (top 7% = HYPER-BOOST while the capacitor has charge), left stick left/right = yaw. Right stick up/down = pitch, right stick left/right = roll. CH5 3-position switch = flight mode, CH6 = split-wings, CH7 = fire.
Plug in: connect the RadioMaster Pocket with a USB-C OTG cable, then pick USB Joystick (HID) on the radio.
Touch: left thumb = throttle (stays where you leave it) + yaw, right thumb = pitch + roll.
Flight modes: ASSIST = gentle auto-level · HORIZON = levels near center, flips at full stick · ACRO = pure rates, no auto-level.
Split-wings: CRUISE (closed) = faster, smaller hitbox. ATTACK (open X) = sharper turns, quad lasers armed.
Skim: fly within a few meters of walls, beams and rocks to trigger PROXIMITY SKIM. That fills the Hyper-Boost capacitor and raises your score multiplier. Touching anything costs shields.
Rings: fly through plasma rings for points, boost and a little shield.
Trench ceiling: climbing out of the Bakal Trench exposes you to flak and drains shields.
